Retaining walls · Wall types

Split-face block retaining walls

Premium tier: structural block with a rough-hewn stone face — permanence and texture without render maintenance.

Photo slotSplit-face block retaining walls — completed, with the drainage or piers visible if possible

How it’s built

Reinforced, core-filled masonry exactly like a Besser block wall, but laid in split-face blocks whose fractured face gives a natural stone texture. Colours from charcoal through sandstone tones.

Where it makes sense

Feature walls, entries and large retaining runs where a rendered wall would look flat. No render means no cracking, no repainting — the finish is the block itself.

Capping and details

Capped with matching block or a contrasting sandstone or concrete capping; corners and steps are detailed in block so the pattern turns cleanly.

Which wall does your block need?

Height, what sits behind it and the look you want decide it — and engineering may decide it for you. Send photos and we’ll tell you which tier fits, and why.
