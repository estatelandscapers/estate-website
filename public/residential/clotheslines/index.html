Where the sun is, not where the builder left a wall.

Clotheslines get bolted wherever is easiest and then hated for years. We position them for sun and access as part of the yard plan, on footings that hold, and install the fold-down or freestanding unit that fits the space.

What we do

Positioned, footed, installed.

Freestanding and fold-down

Rotary, freestanding folding, and wall-mounted fold-down lines from the mainstream ranges, or install one you have bought.

Positioned with the yard

Sun, wind, access from the laundry and the sight line from the entertaining area are decided on the plan, not on the day.

Part of the package

Priced as a line within your landscape package. Installed standalone at sensible scale.

Common questions

Asked before nearly every job.

  • Freestanding or fold-down?

    Fold-down saves space against a fence or wall; freestanding suits larger yards and gives more line. We recommend based on the plan.

  • Will it move?

    Not on our footings. Posts are concreted to the depth the unit needs, which is more than the box says.

  • Can you install one I have bought?

    Yes, as part of the works.

Planning the yard?

Clothesline placement is a five-minute decision on the plan that saves years of annoyance. Ask for it in your quote.
