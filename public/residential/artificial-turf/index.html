What we do

The work, specifically.

The base

Excavation, compacted road base, screeded crusher dust and drainage falls — the same discipline as a paved area, because turf on soft ground ripples within a year.

The install

Weed mat, tensioned turf, glued and pinned seams laid with the pile direction matched, silica sand infill brushed in so the blades stand.

Where it earns its place

Deep shade under trees or beside tall fences, narrow side passages, rooftops and courtyards, and play areas that need to look the same in February as in June.

Common questions

Asked before nearly every job.

  • Real or artificial?

    Real turf where there’s sun and someone to mow; artificial where there isn’t. Most of our yards are real grass, and we’ll say so if that’s the better answer for yours.

  • Does it get hot?

    Yes, in full summer sun — lighter colours and cooling infills help, and it’s another reason it suits shaded areas best.

  • How long does it last?

    Quality turf on a proper base: 10–15 years. On a poor base the turf outlives the surface it sits on.

Shaded yard or side passage?

Photos and rough dimensions are enough. We’ll tell you honestly whether real turf would work instead.
