The process

Seven steps, and you know where you are at each one.

Most complaints about landscapers are about silence, not workmanship. This is what happens and when.

  1. Enquiry

    You tell us the site, the scope and roughly what you're working with. We tell you straight away if it isn't a fit.

  2. Discovery call

    A proper conversation about levels, access, drainage, neighbours and what you actually want to use the space for.

  3. Site visit

    We measure, check falls and access, and photograph the site so the quote is built from the real thing.

  4. Quote in 48 hours

    An itemised quote you can read — element by element, so you can add, cut or stage it.

  5. Contract & scheduling

    Written contract, HBCF cover where the job is over $20,000, and a start date you can plan around.

  6. Construction

    Set-out and levels first, drainage before surfaces, progress photos as we go, and one point of contact throughout.

  7. Handover

    A walk-through, engineer certification where walls need it, and care notes for the turf and planting through their first season.

Common questions

The questions we get asked before a first call.

Straight answers, so you can work out whether to pick up the phone.

  • Which areas of Sydney do you cover?

    We take on residential landscape construction across Sydney, with most of our work in the Hills District and North West. Our commercial team works across New South Wales and Queensland.

  • What is your minimum project size?

    Complete landscape packages generally start from $25,000. Individual engineered or construction elements are assessed by scope. As a guide: retaining walls from around 20 linear metres, turf, paving or garden areas from around 100 square metres.

  • How long does it take to get a quote?

    We call the same day your enquiry arrives, book a site visit, and send an itemised quote within 48 hours of that visit. The quote is broken down element by element so you can add, cut or stage the work.

  • Are you licensed and insured?

    Yes. NSW Fair Trading licence 487076C, $20 million public liability, current workers compensation, and Home Building Compensation Fund cover on residential projects over $20,000. We are members of the Landscape Association, membership 41219.

  • Do you build retaining walls that need engineering?

    Yes. We build engineered retaining walls including excavation, piering, concrete, structural engineering inspections and certification — the documentation you need signed off before an Occupation Certificate.

  • Do you offer garden maintenance, mowing or hedging?

    No. We are a landscape construction team, so ongoing maintenance sits outside what our crews do. Email us anyway and we will introduce you to a maintenance contractor we have vetted.

  • When should I organise landscaping on a new build?

    Talk to us before your builder finishes. Drainage falls, retaining levels and any footings for future structures are far cheaper to get right while machinery can still access the site.

  • Do you install irrigation?

    We install new irrigation as part of a landscape package, designed alongside the planting. We do not take on repairs to existing systems.

Subdividing, or need civil works?

Bulk earthworks, subdivision civil, stormwater infrastructure and road works are delivered by our civil arm.
