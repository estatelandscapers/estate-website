The process

Seven steps, and you know where you are at each one.

Most complaints about landscapers are about silence, not workmanship. This is what happens and when.

  1. Enquiry

    You tell us the site, the scope and roughly what you're working with. We tell you straight away if it isn't a fit.

  2. Discovery call

    A proper conversation about levels, access, drainage, neighbours and what you actually want to use the space for.

  3. Site visit

    We measure, check falls and access, and photograph the site so the quote is built from the real thing.

  4. Quote in 48 hours

    An itemised quote you can read — element by element, so you can add, cut or stage it.

  5. Contract & scheduling

    Written contract, HBCF cover where the job is over $20,000, and a start date you can plan around.

  6. Construction

    Set-out and levels first, drainage before surfaces, progress photos as we go, and one point of contact throughout.

  7. Handover

    A walk-through, engineer certification where walls need it, and care notes for the turf and planting through their first season.

Before you enquire

We're not the right call for every job.

Saying this upfront saves you a week of waiting for a quote that was never coming. If your project is in the right column, we'd rather point you somewhere useful than leave you chasing us.

Work we take on

  • New build and knockdown-rebuild landscape packages
  • Duplex, triplex and multi-dwelling sites
  • Designer-led projects working to a landscape plan
  • Full packages from $25,000
  • Retaining walls from 20 linear metres
  • Turf, paving and garden areas from 100 m²
  • New irrigation installed as part of a package

Work we decline

  • Lawn mowing, hedging and weeding
  • Ongoing garden maintenance
  • Repairs to existing irrigation systems
  • Pressure washing and rubbish removal
  • Small patch or repair jobs
  • Projects under $25,000

If that's what you need, email us anyway — we'll suggest a maintenance contractor who does it properly.
