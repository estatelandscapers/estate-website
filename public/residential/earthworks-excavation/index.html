What we do

The work, specifically.

Site preparation

Stripping, clean-ups, removal of old concrete, stumps and builder’s spoil — a clear, level canvas before anything is built.

Cut, fill & levels

Levels set from the plan and checked, not eyeballed — because 50 mm wrong here becomes a retaining wall problem later.

Access-matched machinery

From tight-access machines through standard excavators; we size the plant to your site rather than the site to our plant. We own our plant and trucks — no waiting on hire availability — and bring in vetted partner operators where a job needs more than we run.

Common questions

Asked before nearly every job.

  • Where does the spoil go?

    Off site, to lawful disposal — it's a line item on the quote, not a surprise. Clean fill can sometimes be reused on site to save cartage.

  • Can you work tight-access blocks?

    Yes. Tight access changes the machinery and the price, not the standard — the quote states which applies.

  • Do you do standalone excavation?

    As part of landscape projects, yes. Bulk civil earthworks and subdivision work sit with our civil arm, Infra Landscapers.

Site need clearing, cutting or levelling?
