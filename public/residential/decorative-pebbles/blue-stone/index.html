Where it works

Right stone, right place.

Best for. Paths, side passages, driveways, drainage strips and under decks. The most economical stone by a distance.

Worth knowing. Crushed, not tumbled, so the edges are sharp. Perfect on a path, less friendly beside a pool.

Common questions

Asked before nearly every job.

  • How deep is Blue Stone laid?

    Roughly twice the stone size: 20 to 40 mm depending on the pebble. Thinner than that and the weedmat shows; thicker wastes stone.

  • Does the colour vary?

    Yes, it is a natural product and shades vary between loads. We order each area from one batch so it matches.

  • Can I mix it with other stone?

    Yes. Feature stones and larger river pebbles are common additions, priced per job.
