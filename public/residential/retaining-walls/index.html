Approvals & certification

When a wall needs an engineer, and paperwork.

As a general rule in NSW, walls over 600 mm, walls close to a boundary, and walls supporting a driveway, structure or neighbouring land need engineering input and may need council or certifier approval. The exact thresholds vary by council and by site, and we confirm them before quoting rather than after building.

  1. Levels & set-out

    Heights, boundary offsets and what the wall actually holds back: measured, not eyeballed.

  2. Engineering detail

    Where the wall requires it, a structural design specifying piers, reinforcement and drainage.

  3. Inspections at hold points

    Piers and steel inspected before concrete is poured, so nothing has to be dug back up to prove itself.

  4. Certification for OC

    The engineer's sign-off, ready for your certifier: the document that keeps a wall from delaying your occupation.

Common questions

Retaining wall questions, answered straight.

  • Do I need council approval for a retaining wall in Sydney?

    Often not for low garden walls, usually yes for anything substantial. In NSW, walls over 600 mm generally fall outside exempt development, and walls near boundaries or supporting loads have stricter rules that vary by council. We check your site's requirements as part of quoting.

  • What makes a retaining wall "engineered"?

    A structural engineer designs it for the actual load: pier depth and spacing, reinforcement, concrete strength and drainage. The engineer then inspects at hold points during construction and certifies the finished wall.

  • Why do retaining walls fail?

    Almost always from behind: water building up because there's no drainage, or footings too shallow for the load. That's why every wall we build gets an ag line, gravel backfill and falls that move water away from it.

  • What decides the cost of a wall?

    Height, length, what it retains, ground conditions, and access, a wall a machine can reach costs meaningfully less than one built by barrow. An itemised quote shows each element, so you can see what drives the number.

  • Is there a minimum size you take on?

    Around 20 linear metres as a standalone job. Shorter walls we build all the time: as part of a full landscape package.

  • Do you provide the certification my certifier asks for?

    Yes. Where a wall is engineered, the structural inspections and final certification are part of the job, handed over ready for your Occupation Certificate.
