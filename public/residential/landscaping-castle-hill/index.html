Common questions

Castle Hill questions, answered straight.

  • Do you service Castle Hill?

    Yes — Castle Hill is core service area for our residential crews, based nearby in Rouse Hill. Complete landscape packages generally start from $25,000. Individual engineered or construction elements are assessed by scope. Standalone retaining, turf or paving is taken on at the scales listed on our services pages.

  • Do I need council approval for landscaping in Castle Hill?

    Castle Hill sits in the The Hills Shire council area. Low garden walls and many landscape works need no approval; larger retaining walls, structures and significant earthworks can — thresholds vary with height, boundaries and site. We confirm what your project needs as part of quoting.

  • How fast can you quote?

    We call the same business day, book a site visit, and the itemised quote arrives within 48 hours of the visit.
