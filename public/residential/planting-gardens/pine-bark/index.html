Common questions

Asked before nearly every job.

  • Is Pine Bark right for natives?

    Tea tree and leaf mulch suit native beds best; pine bark and cypress are fine on most. We match mulch to the planting.

  • Can I mix tiers in one yard?

    Yes. A premium soil in the feature bed and standard elsewhere is common and is priced bed by bed.

  • How much do I need?

    We measure the beds at the site visit and the quote carries the volume. Nothing to calculate.

Planting beds this season?

Photos and the plan are enough. Soil, mulch and plants are lines on the quote at the tier you choose.
