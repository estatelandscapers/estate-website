Privacy policy

How we handle your information.

Apexx Enterprises Pty Ltd trading as Estate Landscapers (ABN 94 636 443 108) is committed to handling personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). Last updated August 2026.

  • What we collect

    When you enquire through this website we collect what you provide: name, contact details, project address and details, and any files you upload (such as plans and site photos). Our website also collects standard analytics data via Google Analytics — pages visited, device type and approximate location — which does not identify you personally.

  • Why we collect it

    To respond to your enquiry, prepare quotes and tender submissions, deliver and document our work, and meet our legal and insurance obligations. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your details for third-party marketing.

  • Where it’s stored

    Enquiry details are held in our lead and quoting system. Uploaded files are stored in our Microsoft OneDrive business storage. Emails are handled through our business email providers. These providers may store data on servers inside and outside Australia under their own security standards.

  • Who we share it with

    Only those needed to deliver the work: our team, and where a project requires it, engineers, certifiers or subcontractors engaged on your job. If your enquiry is outside our scope, we only refer your details to another contractor with your agreement.

  • Access, correction and deletion

    You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (subject to records we’re required to keep, such as contracted works). Email enquiry@estatelandscapers.com.au and we’ll respond promptly.

  • Cookies and analytics

    We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used. You can block or clear cookies in your browser without affecting your ability to contact us.

  • Questions or complaints

    Contact us first at the email above. If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
