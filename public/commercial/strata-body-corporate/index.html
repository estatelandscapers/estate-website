How we work with you

Built around how your side of the table runs.

A scope you can vote on

Itemised, in plain language, with options priced separately — so the committee decides between real alternatives instead of a single lump sum.

Occupied-site discipline

Access maintained, work areas contained, notice given — residents live there while we work, and the job is sequenced around that fact.

Records for the scheme

Insurances and licences upfront, progress photos through the works, certification where retaining or structures require it — everything the strata manager files.

Common questions

Asked before most engagements.

  • Can you work with our strata manager?

    Yes — most engagements run through the managing agent, with quotes structured for committee papers and invoices structured for the plan’s accounts.

  • Our retaining wall is failing — where do we start?

    With an inspection and an honest scope: what’s failing, why, and what repair versus rebuild costs. Engineered walls get engineering — certification is part of the job.

  • Do you do ongoing garden maintenance for schemes?

    No — we build and remediate. For ongoing maintenance we’ll refer a contractor who does it well, and the two scopes stay clean.

Committee weighing up landscape works?

Send photos and what the scheme wants to achieve. We’ll return a scope written for the meeting, not for a filing cabinet.
