Constraints

What made it worth doing properly.

The scope covered both landscape and hardscape works around a residential site, with neighbour-side access needed for soil movement and a substantial new driveway/backyard concrete component.

Boundary / neighbour coordinationTight program or OC deadlineCouncil / DA / CDC conditions

Western-neighbour side access was required to move soil into the rear yard. The site plan identifies 239.27 m² of landscaping, with the scope also extending to driveway/backyard concrete and front boundary finishes.

How we approached it

Sequenced so nothing gets done twice.

Estate combined Buffalo turf and garden-bed finishes with fencing, gate and aluminium front fence, then coordinated new concrete, white marble/pavers, brick edging and the front feature wall.

Worth noting

The site plan records landscaping. The concrete scope included driveway/backyard concrete, with a charcoal-colour and sealer upgrade to the driveway after curing.
