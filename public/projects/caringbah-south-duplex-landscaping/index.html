Constraints

What made it worth doing properly.

The project combined a large softscape area with concrete crossover/driveway works and neighbour-side access for soil movement. Drainage also needed a level-spreader detail with certified plumbing components.

Boundary / neighbour coordinationExisting services (gas, water, comms)Poor drainage / water issuesTight program or OC deadlineCouncil / DA / CDC conditions

Neighbour-side access was required to move soil into the rear yards. The package also included a certified level-spreader drainage detail and a council-compliant plain concrete crossover.

How we approached it

Sequenced so nothing gets done twice.

Estate coordinated Buffalo turf, garden-bed mix, 69 m of Colorbond fencing, two gates and 128 installed plants with concrete, white pebbles, edging, irrigation and the level-spreader/drainage works.

Worth noting

The drainage scope included a level spreader using concrete, geofabric, blue metal and subsoil, together with a Class B 200 mm drain connection and plumber certification. The plain concrete crossover was specified to council requirements.

Common questions

About this project.

  • What was included in this Caringbah South duplex landscape?

    The scope included DNA-certified Buffalo turf, premium garden-bed mix, 69 m of Colorbond fencing, two gates and installation of 128 plants. It also included decorative pebbles, edging and concrete access works.

  • What drainage work was completed at Caringbah South?

    The project included a level-spreader system with concrete, geofabric, blue metal and subsoil, plus a Class B 200 mm drain connection to a pit. A plumber certificate formed part of the level-spreader scope.

  • What concrete work was included in the duplex landscaping?

    Estate allowed for throw-on coloured driveway concrete and plain concrete crossover/steps to council requirements. The existing crossover was also demolished and removed.
