Constraints

What made it worth doing properly.

The large block has a pronounced level change, with the site plan stepping through multiple finished levels. The landscape package had to coordinate walls, floating steps, driveway and walkways while maintaining access through the site.

Steep slope / significant fallBoundary / neighbour coordinationTight program or OC deadlineCouncil / DA / CDC conditions

The site plan shows substantial level change across the block. Full-time access to the property, driveway and a neighbour's side yard was required for the works.

How we approached it

Sequenced so nothing gets done twice.

Estate sequenced the cleanup and block levelling before constructing 63 m of garden-bed/front rendered walls, floating steps and driveway/walkway concrete, then finished the project with Buffalo turf and 22 m of Colorbond fencing.

Worth noting

The concrete package combined throw-on coloured driveway with plain-grey walkways and level-change steps. The garden walls were up to 0.6 m high with excavation and footings included.

Common questions

About this project.

  • What was included in this North Kellyville boutique home landscape?

    The package included 63 m of garden-bed and front rendered walls, architectural-mix floating steps, driveway and walkway concrete, Buffalo turf and 22 m of Colorbond fencing.

  • How was the level change handled on the North Kellyville site?

    The hardscape was split between garden walls, floating steps and graded concrete access surfaces. This created practical transitions across the steep block while keeping the landscape finishes coordinated.

  • What concrete finishes were used at the North Kellyville home?

    The driveway used a throw-on coloured concrete finish across, while walkways and level-change steps were plain grey. The floating steps used an architectural concrete mix.
