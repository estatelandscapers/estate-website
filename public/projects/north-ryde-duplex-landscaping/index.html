Constraints

What made it worth doing properly.

The project combined demolition, new concrete and landscaping around a duplex site, with fence works dependent on neighbour access timing. A backyard sleeper wall and heavy-duty driveway trench drain added technical coordination to the scope.

Boundary / neighbour coordinationTight program or OC deadlineCouncil / DA / CDC conditions

The package also included driveway/crossover works and a backyard sleeper retaining wall.

How we approached it

Sequenced so nothing gets done twice.

Estate removed the palm/shrubs and existing driveway/kerb, then installed Buffalo turf, garden finishes, decorative stone, 103 m of Colorbond fencing, two gates and new concrete with integrated trench drainage.

Worth noting

The backyard retaining element used 75 mm timber sleepers with galvanised steel posts over 15.2 m. The driveway concrete incorporated a 150 mm heavy-duty galvanised trench drain, alongside new kerb/layback and backyard steps.

Common questions

About this project.

  • What was included in this North Ryde duplex landscaping package?

    The project included Buffalo turf, garden-bed mix and mulch, decorative stone, 103 m of Colorbond fencing and two gates. Estate also delivered driveway/backyard concrete, steps, retaining and practical site items.

  • What retaining wall was installed in the North Ryde backyard?

    A 15.2 m timber sleeper wall was included using 75 mm sleepers with galvanised steel posts. The plan notes a height of up to approximately 0.6 m.

  • How was driveway drainage handled at North Ryde?

    The new driveway and backyard concrete scope included a 150 mm heavy-duty galvanised trench drain. This was integrated into the concrete package to manage water at the driveway.
