Questions, answered straight.

The things people ask before their first call.

What does Estate Landscapers do?

We are a licensed landscape construction company (NSW Fair Trading licence 487076C). We design and build complete landscapes for homes across Greater Sydney, including retaining walls, driveways, fencing, paving, pool surrounds, turf, planting, lighting and irrigation, and we deliver commercial landscape packages for builders, developers, councils and strata across NSW and Queensland.

Which areas do you work in?

Residential projects across Greater Sydney, including the Hills District, the North Shore, the Northern Beaches, the Eastern Suburbs, the Inner West, Parramatta and Ryde, St George and the Sutherland Shire. Commercial projects across NSW and Queensland.

What size of project do you take on?

Complete residential landscape packages generally start from $25,000. We also take on smaller single-element jobs from about $10,000. Under that, we will tell you honestly that a smaller crew is likely to serve you better.

How much does landscaping cost in Sydney?

Most complete packages for a new home on a standard block land between $45,000 and $80,000, and large, sloping or premium-finish properties run from $100,000 upward. Our online estimate gives you a range for your own yard at three levels of finish in under ten minutes.

Do your retaining walls need an engineer?

Every retaining wall above 600 mm needs an engineer's sign-off, and we arrange it as part of the job. Walls above 1.2 m are a different class of work that needs heavy engineering and may need a geotechnical report.

How quickly can I get a quote?

Start with the online estimate for a same-minute ballpark. Site visits are on Fridays, and the itemised quote follows within 48 hours of the visit.
