Besser block retaining walls

The Premium family: core-filled, reinforced masonry on an engineered footing — a permanent wall, rendered or left raw.

Photo slotBesser block retaining walls — completed, with the drainage or piers visible if possible

How it’s built

Hollow concrete blocks laid on a reinforced concrete footing, with vertical steel through the cores and every core filled with concrete. Engineered to the load behind it; with drainage and waterproofing on the retained side.

Where it makes sense

Higher walls, walls carrying surcharge from driveways, pools or structures, and anything that must be permanent. Besser block is the structural base for both the rendered and split-face walls in our Premium tier.

Finish options

Left raw for an industrial look, rendered and painted for a clean architectural finish, or built in split-face block for texture — the structure is the same, the face is the choice.

Which wall does your block need?

Height, what sits behind it and the look you want decide it — and engineering may decide it for you. Send photos and we’ll tell you which tier fits, and why.
