Timber sleeper retaining walls with steel posts

The Basic tier: galvanised steel posts set in concrete with treated timber sleepers — the most affordable engineered wall, with an honest lifespan.

How it’s built

Galvanised steel H- or C-section posts are concreted into piers at engineered centres; treated pine or hardwood sleepers slide in between. Behind the wall goes ag pipe in a gravel drain — the part that decides whether the wall lasts half its life or all of it.

Where it makes sense

Garden terracing, level changes up to around a metre, and budgets where the money is better spent on drainage and preparation than on the face material. It’s the wall system in our Basic tier.

Honest limits

Timber is organic: expect 15–20 years from treated pine, longer from hardwood, less where the wall stays wet. Above certain heights, or under surcharge from a driveway or structure, engineering will push you to concrete or block regardless of budget.
