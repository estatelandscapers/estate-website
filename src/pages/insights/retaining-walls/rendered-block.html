The Premium tier finish: an engineered block wall rendered smooth and painted to match the house — the architectural option.

How it’s built

A reinforced, core-filled Besser block wall on an engineered footing, waterproofed on the retained side, then rendered with a cement or acrylic render and painted. Drainage behind the wall protects both the structure and the finish.

Where it makes sense

Modern homes where the wall should read as part of the architecture — matched to the house colour, sharp edges, integrated steps and planter walls. It’s the specification in our Premium retaining tier.

Maintenance

Render and paint will want recoating over the years, and hairline cracking at joints is normal in masonry; correct waterproofing and drainage keep it cosmetic rather than structural.

Which wall does your block need?

Height, what sits behind it and the look you want decide it — and engineering may decide it for you. Send photos and we’ll tell you which tier fits, and why.
