Decorative concrete sleeper retaining walls

Standard-tier structure with a stone, timber-grain or ribbed face — the concrete wall that doesn’t look like one.

Photo slotDecorative concrete sleeper retaining walls — completed, with the drainage or piers visible if possible

How it’s built

Identical engineering to a plain concrete sleeper wall — steel posts in piers, reinforced sleepers, drained backfill — with sleepers cast in a patterned face: sandstone, timber-grain, split-stone or ribbed, in several colours.

Where it makes sense

Front-of-house walls and anywhere the wall is part of the view. You get a 40-year concrete wall with a finish that reads as stone or timber, for a modest step up from plain.

Watch-outs

Patterns and colours vary by supplier and can change; we confirm samples before ordering so the face you approve is the face that arrives.

Which wall does your block need?

Height, what sits behind it and the look you want decide it — and engineering may decide it for you. Send photos and we’ll tell you which tier fits, and why.
