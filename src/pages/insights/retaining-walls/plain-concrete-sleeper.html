Plain concrete sleeper retaining walls

The Standard tier: reinforced concrete sleepers in steel posts — timber’s ease of build with a 40-year-plus lifespan.

Photo slotPlain concrete sleeper retaining walls — completed, with the drainage or piers visible if possible

How it’s built

The same post-and-panel system as timber, but the sleepers are steel-reinforced concrete: 40+ years, no rot, no termites, no movement from moisture. Posts are concreted into engineered piers; drainage behind is non-negotiable.

Where it makes sense

The default for most Sydney yards: boundary walls, driveways cut into slope, and any wall you don’t want to think about again. It’s the wall system in our Standard tier.

Finish

Smooth grey face straight off the truck. Cost-effective and neutral — and if you want texture, the decorative version uses the same structure with a patterned face.

Which wall does your block need?

Height, what sits behind it and the look you want decide it — and engineering may decide it for you. Send photos and we’ll tell you which tier fits, and why.
