Constraints

What made it worth doing properly.

The compact inner-city site had constrained access through the house and/or Goddard Reserve. The raised planter scope also needed coordination around exclusions for excavation, contaminated soil and stormwater works.

Narrow or restricted accessExisting services (gas, water, comms)Council / DA / CDC conditions

Access was through the house(s) and/or Goddard Reserve. Planter excavation, contaminated-soil removal, stormwater drainage and engineering/council costs were excluded from the quoted landscape scope.

How we approached it

Sequenced so nothing gets done twice.

Estate completed the cleanup, 17 m of corten edging, planter mix, Buffalo turf and installation of 76 plants, then added drainage/soil to the Level 1 planter and drip irrigation to six beds.

Worth noting

The Level 1 planter was a raised landscape element with drainage and soil included. The irrigation was specified as low-pressure drip to six planter beds, with tap connections by others.
