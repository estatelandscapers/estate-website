Common questions

About this project.

  • How many plants were installed in the Kemps Creek vertical green walls?

    The project included 10 vertical green walls with approximately 300–600 plants on each wall. That equates to roughly 3,000–6,000 plant positions across the full living-wall installation.

  • What services supported the vertical green walls?

    The wall system included irrigation and a dosing system to support the planting. The wider scope also included ground-level planting, turf, garden beds and retaining walls.

  • Was maintenance included after the green walls were installed?

    Yes. The project included one year of maintenance after installation, providing ongoing care for the vertical green walls and associated landscape areas.
