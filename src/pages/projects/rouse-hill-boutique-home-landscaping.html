Common questions

About this project.

  • What landscaping was completed at this Rouse Hill boutique home?

    The package included turf, pebbled landscape areas, 36.5 m of boundary fencing, two gates, aluminium edging and front stepping/timber steps. A composite deck with timber pergola formed the main outdoor-living feature.

  • How was the new fence connected around the existing retaining wall?

    The fence scope required core cutting into the existing retaining-wall zone and installation of four powder-coated posts. Additional sleepers were also installed beneath sections of the fence.

  • Why was part of the turf installed specifically for OC?

    The scope separately identified Buffalo turf for OC presentation, in addition to the main turf installation. This allowed the external areas to be finished as part of the home-completion works.
