Constraints

What made it worth doing properly.

The site had a significant fall and the boundary condition required a 41 m retaining wall up to 1.2 m high, soil removal and access to both the client and neighbouring property. The wall also required drainage plus structural engineer design, site inspection and approval.

Steep slope / significant fallBoundary / neighbour coordinationExisting services (gas, water, comms)Shared driveway or easementCouncil / DA / CDC conditionsEngineering & certification required

Boundary/easement works required coordination with the neighbouring property and access to both properties. Earthworks included relocating soil to the backyard, plus approximately 60 tonnes of off-site spoil removal for the retaining-wall variation.

How we approached it

Sequenced so nothing gets done twice.

Estate coordinated the earthworks, soil relocation and off-site spoil removal with the retaining-wall installation, drainage and engineering approval, while delivering the wider duplex landscape package. The completed scope included 66 m of Colorbond fencing, four gates, Sir Walter Buffalo turf, decorative pebbles and garden finishes.

Worth noting

Retaining-wall variation: 41 m long and up to 1.2 m high using plain grey Ezzy Fit composite sleepers with galvanized steel posts. Agline and aggregate backfill were included for drainage, with structural engineer design, site inspection and sign-off.

Common questions

About this project.

  • What was included in this duplex landscaping project in Ermington?

    The scope included certified Sir Walter Buffalo turf with underlay, river pebbles over weedmat, garden mix and mulch, 66 m of 1.8 m Colorbond fencing, four gates, two letterboxes and two freestanding clotheslines. A 41 m boundary retaining wall was added as a variation.

  • How was the 41 m boundary retaining wall built and drained?

    The variation allowed for a 41 m plain grey Ezzy Fit retaining wall up to 1.2 m high with galvanized steel posts. Agline and aggregate backfill were included for drainage, with structural engineer design, site inspection and sign-off.

  • How much excavation and soil removal was required at the Ermington site?

    The retaining-wall variation included removal and disposal of soil. The original landscape scope also included moving soil from the front yard to the rear, then spreading and levelling it.
