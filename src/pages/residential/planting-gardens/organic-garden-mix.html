Common questions

Asked before nearly every job.

  • Is Organic Garden Mix right for natives?

    Native Mix is the safe choice for natives, azaleas and camellias because it carries no mushroom compost and drains freely. We specify it wherever the plant schedule calls for it, at any tier.

  • Can I mix tiers in one yard?

    Yes. A premium soil in the feature bed and standard elsewhere is common and is priced bed by bed.

  • How much do I need?

    We measure the beds at the site visit and the quote carries the volume. Nothing to calculate.
