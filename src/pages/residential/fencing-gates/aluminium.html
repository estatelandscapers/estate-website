Common questions

Asked before nearly every job.

  • Does the fence follow a sloping block?

    Yes. Panels are stepped or raked to the ground line, and plinths close the gap underneath so soil and mulch stay on your side.

  • Who pays for a boundary fence?

    Dividing fences are usually shared between neighbours under NSW fencing law. We build to your instruction and can supply the quote you need for that conversation.

  • Can you match an existing fence?

    Usually. Colorbond colours are standard, and timber profiles are common. Send a photo.

Fencing a boundary in aluminium?

Send the run length and a photo of the line. It is a line on your quote at tier, gates included.
