Common questions

Asked before nearly every job.

  • Why do driveways crack?

    Concrete always moves; the question is where. Poor base compaction and missing control joints let it crack randomly. A compacted base and planned joints keep movement where it's designed to be.

  • Plain, coloured or exposed aggregate?

    Plain is the workhorse; colour and exposed aggregate lift the front of the house for a known extra cost per square metre — the itemised quote shows the difference so you can decide.

  • Do you do small repair pours?

    Standalone patch and repair jobs, no — they sit with our referral partners. Concrete elements inside a landscape package, always.
