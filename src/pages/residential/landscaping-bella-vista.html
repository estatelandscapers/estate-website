How a project runs

From first photos to handover.

  1. Online estimate. Answer a few plain questions about the yard and see a ballpark range from Basic to Premium finishes in under ten minutes.
  2. Friday site visit. We measure, check the access and the falls, and photograph the site.
  3. Itemised quote within 48 hours. Every element is a line, priced at three tiers, with the approvals, engineering and certification the job needs stated up front.
  4. Build in sequence. Excavation and drainage first, then walls and concrete, then fencing, soil, turf and planting, so nothing is done twice.
  5. Handover. The paperwork your certifier asks for is handed over with the yard.

Common questions

Bella Vista questions, answered straight.

  • Do you service Bella Vista?

    Yes. Bella Vista is core service area for our residential crews, based nearby in Rouse Hill. Complete landscape packages generally start from $25,000. Individual engineered or construction elements are assessed by scope. Standalone retaining, turf or paving is taken on at the scales listed on our services pages.

  • Do I need council approval for landscaping in Bella Vista?

    Bella Vista sits in the The Hills Shire council area. Low garden walls and many landscape works need no approval; larger retaining walls, structures and significant earthworks can, thresholds vary with height, boundaries and site. We confirm what your project needs as part of quoting.

  • How fast can you quote?

    We call the same business day, book a site visit, and the itemised quote arrives within 48 hours of the visit.

  • What does a landscaping project in Bella Vista usually include?

    Most are full packages: retaining and levels, drainage, driveway or paths, fencing, soil, turf and planting, priced as one itemised quote. Decks, pergolas and pool surrounds are added as separate lines.

  • Are retaining walls engineered?

    Structural retaining walls are built to an engineer's design and signed off by the engineer, and you keep that paperwork for your certifier, your insurer and any future sale.

  • Can I see a finished project before deciding?

    Yes. Our Rouse Hill, North Kellyville and Dural projects are on the Projects page with the scope of each, and they are the closest completed work to Bella Vista.
