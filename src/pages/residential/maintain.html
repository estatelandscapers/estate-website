What we do

The work, specifically.

Establishment programs

The first months decide whether turf and plants thrive. Watering, first mows, feeding and pest checks scheduled from handover, not left to chance.

Scheduled maintenance

Monthly or seasonal visits: mowing, hedging, pruning, mulch top-ups, irrigation checks and drainage clean-outs — by people who built it and know it.

Only our projects

We maintain the landscapes we construct, and only those. Maintaining someone else’s build means inheriting their shortcuts — we’d rather keep our standard consistent.

Common questions

Asked before nearly every job.

  • Do you maintain gardens you didn’t build?

    No. Maintenance is offered on Estate-built projects only, so the crew, the knowledge and the quality stay consistent. For other yards we’ll gladly refer a vetted maintenance contractor.

  • When does maintenance start?

    Ideally at handover, with an establishment program covering the first season — the period where new turf and planting are most vulnerable.

  • Can I stop and start?

    Yes. Programs run monthly, seasonally or as a one-off pre-sale or pre-event tidy, and you can change the cadence as the landscape matures.
