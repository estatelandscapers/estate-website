What we do

Supplied, set, finished.

Supply and install

Pillar, post-mounted or wall-mounted letterboxes from the mainstream ranges, or install one you have bought.

Set correctly

Plumb, level, at the required height and clear of the driveway sight line, on a concrete footing that will not move.

Part of the package

Priced as a line within your landscape package. Installed standalone at sensible scale, for example alongside a fence or driveway job.

Common questions

Asked before nearly every job.

  • Can you install a letterbox I have already bought?

    Yes. Bring it to site and we set it as part of the works.

  • Is there a height rule?

    Australia Post specifies a letter slot height range and clear access. We set to it so deliveries are never an issue.

  • Do you do letterboxes on their own?

    At sensible scale, yes; typically with a fence, driveway or front-yard job rather than as a single trip.
