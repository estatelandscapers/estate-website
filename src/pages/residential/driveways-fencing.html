Driveway and fence questions.

Which driveway finish lasts best?

All three finishes last for decades when the base is right. Exposed aggregate hides wear and tyre marks best and has the most texture underfoot. Colour concrete gives a cleaner modern look. Plain concrete is the most economical. The base preparation, steel and joints decide the life of the slab more than the finish does.

Do I need council approval for a new driveway?

The section on council land between your boundary and the road, the crossover, needs council approval and inspections. We manage that application as part of the job. The driveway within your property is usually exempt, but we confirm what applies to your site when we quote.

How high can a front fence be without approval in NSW?

Generally 1.2 m at the front and 1.8 m on the side and rear boundaries under the exempt development rules, with conditions on materials, corner lots and slopes. Our plain-English guide to fence heights sets out the details.

Can you build the driveway and fencing as part of a bigger landscape?

Yes, and that is how most of our driveways and fences are built. Doing them with the levels, retaining and drainage means everything falls the right way and nothing is dug up twice.
