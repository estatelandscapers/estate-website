What we do

The work, specifically.

Timber

H4 treated timber, pegged and set to string lines — the cost-effective edge that disappears into a mulched bed.

Charcoal brick

A laid brick mowing strip: lets the mower run over the edge, holds turf back from beds, and gives a crisp dark line.

Corten steel

Thin, sharp, curves that timber can’t make, and a rust patina that gets better with age — the premium edge for designed yards.

Common questions

Asked before nearly every job.

  • Why does my current edging look messy?

    Usually it wasn’t set to level, or the turf was laid over it. We install edging before turf and gravel, to a string line, and fix it at the depth the material needs.

  • Can edging curve?

    Corten steel and brick handle curves cleanly; timber suits straight runs and gentle angles.

  • Is it priced separately?

    Edging is a line on your quote, priced per metre at the tier you choose — you can see exactly what the upgrade from timber to steel costs.
