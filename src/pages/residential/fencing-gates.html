What we do

The work, specifically.

Boundary fencing

Colorbond and timber on properly set posts, with concrete plinths under the line where levels differ, so soil stays on your side and the fence stays straight.

Front fencing & screening

Blade fencing, aluminium slats and privacy screens that match the house, usually built with the front landscape so footings and finishes align.

Gates

Common questions

Asked before nearly every job.

  • Colorbond, aluminium or timber?

    Colorbond for boundaries: durable and cost-effective. Aluminium slat for fronts and screening: sharp and low-maintenance. Timber where warmth matters and upkeep is accepted.

  • What about the neighbour’s share?

    Dividing fences are commonly shared under NSW dividing-fence arrangements. We quote the whole job; how it’s split is between neighbours, and an itemised quote makes that conversation easier.

  • Fence on a retaining wall?

    Yes: engineered correctly, with the wall designed to carry the fence load. That combination is exactly where cheap builds fail.
